# Listing photographs

The artifact leaves three photo frames on each lot page. Drop files here with these exact names and
they appear automatically on reload:

```
lot1-1.jpg  lot1-2.jpg  lot1-3.jpg     1127 Saddle Horn Bend
lot2-1.jpg  lot2-2.jpg  lot2-3.jpg     336 Wagon Wheel Trail W
lot3-1.jpg  lot3-2.jpg  lot3-3.jpg     Lot 29 Broken Arrow Trail
lot4-1.jpg  lot4-2.jpg  lot4-3.jpg     750 Wagon Wheel Trail
```

Frame 1 is captioned *waterfront view*, frame 2 *lot / frontage*, frame 3 *street / access*. Roughly
4:3 and about 1,200 px wide is plenty; the frames crop to fill.

## Why they are not already here

Photographs on Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com and Homes.com are licensed to those platforms and to the
listing brokerage, and those sites block direct linking from other pages. Redistributing them in this
repository would be a licensing problem, and hotlinking them would simply show broken images. So the
artifact links to each listing gallery instead, and gives you these frames to fill from your own
download.

What *is* embedded live, and needs no permission, is **Google Maps satellite imagery** — one live map
per lot plus a combined interactive map of all four (§5), through Google's own keyless embed. The lot
pages also carry buttons through to Google Street View. Alongside that, §11–15 draw each lot at true
scale from the county's own LiDAR contours, which shows the waterfront and the fall to the water more
usefully than a marketing photograph does.

Every lot page also carries a **Zillow search link** for its address, so the current listing and its
gallery are one click away even after the listing ID changes.
